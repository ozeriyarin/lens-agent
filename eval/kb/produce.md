# Produce (sample knowledge base for the evals)

## Eggplants
Eggplants keep about five days in the vegetable drawer. Use them first when they go soft or the
skin wrinkles. Best roasted whole over the gas flame for baba ganoush.

## Lettuce and arugula
Bagged romaine and arugula last four to six days once opened. Keep them in the drawer with the
humidity control slider set to high. Wash only right before eating.

## Yogurt
GO by Yoplait is the protein yogurt for after the gym, vanilla flavour. The labneh tub is for
breakfast; it keeps a week after opening.
