# House notes (sample knowledge base for the evals)

## Beer
The Heineken bottles in the fridge door are Dana's. Keep at least two cold; she drinks one on
Friday evenings. Do not put them in the freezer.

## Eggs
Eggs come from the Saturday market in cartons of six. They keep about three weeks in the door
holder. The pencil mark on a shell is the market's date stamp, not damage.

## Tahini
The big jar with the red lid is raw tahini from Har Bracha. Once opened it keeps for two months
in the fridge; stir before use because the oil separates.

## Mugs
The white ceramic mug with the blue rim and the chipped handle is Dana's favourite mug. Do not
put it in the dishwasher.
